<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Overview</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">CineV helps you create animated videos with a clear, step-by-step workflow. It is designed for precise editing, so you can shape each scene with more control and less guesswork.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This guide walks you through the recommended flow: create a storyboard, refine images with tools like PoseShot, Style Enhancer, and Mark Up Image, animate your shots, combine clips and sound in Cut &amp; Mix, and export the final film. If you are getting started with CineV, this is the best place to begin.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">FAQ</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">What is a Storyboard?</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A storyboard visualizes your story and directing ideas.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In CINEV, your written story is automatically turned into scene-by-scene shots, showing camera angles, character positions, and action flow.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can preview how your story will look on screen, edit or add shots, and move directly into video production.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How do I save and export my video?</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In Cut &amp; Mix, add the clips you want to the timeline, save the final sequence, then click Export to export your video.</p>
