Find answers to common questions from webtoon artists and IP holders.

Can I use my own original characters in CineV?

Yes. Upload your original character as a VRM file or image. CineV locks in the look so your character appears consistently across every scene and episode — no prompt variation, no drift.

Do I need animation or 3D skills to use CineV?

No. CineV’s 3D virtual set lets you direct movement, gaze, and camera angles through an intuitive browser interface. If you can point and click, you can direct a scene. No coding or prior animation experience required.

Can I really publish an anime series as a solo creator?

Yes — that’s exactly what CineV is built for. You write the story, CineV generates the script and scene setup, you direct and export each episode. Build your series one episode at a time, on your own schedule.

Does CineV support ZEPETO avatars and VRM characters?

Yes. Log in with your ZEPETO account to import avatars directly. VRM files from any source are also fully supported. You can test different characters for the same performance and cast the perfect fit.

Does CineV work on low-spec hardware?

Yes. Everything runs in the cloud — your local hardware specs don’t matter at all. Any modern browser on any device is all you need to direct and produce your series.

Is CineV free to start?

Yes. You get 1,500 credits instantly on sign-up — no credit card required. Earn additional credits by completing a short survey after your first export. Start your first episode today at zero cost.
