<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the Article Covers</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">South Korea&#8217;s leading daily reported on a structural shift happening in the global animation industry — one that CineV is built to accelerate.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>AI now handles up to 95% of production</strong> — Japan&#8217;s <em>Twins Hinahima</em> became the first AI-produced animation to air on a Japanese broadcast network (MBS), with AI automating line art, coloring, lip-sync, and character movement</li>



<li><strong>Production time cut in half</strong> — A standard 12-episode series that once took two or more years can now be completed in roughly half that time</li>



<li><strong>Cost savings of 60–70%</strong> — Industry insiders estimate AI reduces production cost and time by that margin when planning and direction are excluded</li>



<li><strong>CineV cited directly</strong> — The article highlights CineV as a Korean solution where users input a story and AI builds it into a 3D cinematic video — with full control over acting, lighting, camera angle, and background</li>



<li><strong>China is moving fast too</strong> — State broadcaster CCTV aired a fully AI-generated animation series; Chinese AI startups are targeting 100 manga titles this year with teams as small as 5 people</li>
</ul>
