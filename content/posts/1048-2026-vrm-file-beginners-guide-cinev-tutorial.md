<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You found a VRM file somewhere, or someone told you to &#8220;just use VRM.&#8221; But you have no idea what that means, and you definitely have not made an animation before.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">That is fine. This guide starts from zero.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Is a VRM File?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A VRM file is a 3D character file. Think of it as a container that holds a character&#8217;s appearance, body proportions, and movement settings, all in one place.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The format was originally built for virtual avatars (VTubers, VRChat, virtual meetings), but it has become a common starting point for animation as well. If you have a <code><strong>.vrm</strong></code> file, you have a character ready to move.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Where Do You Get a VRM File?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You have two options depending on whether you have your own character or not.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Option A: Make one with VRoid Studio (free)</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">VRoid Studio is a free character creation app by Pixiv. You pick a face, hair, outfit, and body type using sliders, no drawing skill needed. When you are done, you export the character as a <code>.vrm</code> file.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Download VRoid Studio: <a href="https://vroid.com/en/studio">vroid.com/en/studio</a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The export process takes about two clicks. Go to <strong>Camera/Exporter</strong>, select <strong>Export</strong>, and save the file to your desktop.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Option B: Download a free VRM model</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Several platforms distribute free VRM models made by other creators:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><a href="https://hub.vroid.com/en">VRoid Hub</a> — the largest library, many models are free to use for personal projects</li>



<li><a href="https://booth.pm">Booth.pm</a> — search &#8220;VRM free&#8221; for community-made characters</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Check the usage terms on each model before you use it. Most free models allow personal and creative use.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How to Upload Your VRM to CineV</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once you have a <code>.vrm</code> file, the next step is getting it into CineV.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">First, create a free account at <a href="https://cinev.com">cinev.com</a>. New accounts receive 300 credits automatically. No credit card required.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1. Go to &#8220;My Character&#8221;</h3>



<div class="wp-block-uagb-image uagb-block-19e12956 wp-block-uagb-image--layout-default wp-block-uagb-image--effect-static wp-block-uagb-image--align-none"><figure class="wp-block-uagb-image__figure"><img decoding="async" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/register_my_character.png ,/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/register_my_character.png 780w, /wp-content/uploads/2026/03/register_my_character.png 360w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 480px) 150px" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/register_my_character.png" alt="CineV character registration screen showing an original anime IP character being uploaded as VRM and previewed on a 3D stage" class="uag-image-428" width="609" height="387" title="register_my_character" loading="lazy" role="img"/></figure></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After logging in, open the <strong>My Character</strong> section from the left sidebar. This is where CineV stores all your character files.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2. Upload your VRM file</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>



<div class="wp-block-uagb-image uagb-block-6fd62d31 wp-block-uagb-image--layout-default wp-block-uagb-image--effect-static wp-block-uagb-image--align-none"><figure class="wp-block-uagb-image__figure"><img decoding="async" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upload_my_character.png ,/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upload_my_character.png 780w, /wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upload_my_character.png 360w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 480px) 150px" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upload_my_character.png" alt="Click Add Character, then select Upload VRM. CineV reads the VRM data and builds a preview of your character. This usually takes under a minute." class="uag-image-1054" width="636" height="404" title="upload_my_character" loading="lazy" role="img"/></figure></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Click <strong>Add Character</strong>, then select <strong>Upload VRM</strong>. CineV reads the VRM data and builds a preview of your character. This usually takes under a minute.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If your character looks different from what you expected, check the <a href="https://help.cinev.com/en/articles/VRM-Character-Upload-Guide-d0c2d97f">VRM Character Upload Guide</a> in the help center for common fixes.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3. Set your character as the protagonist</h3>



<div class="wp-block-uagb-image uagb-block-a666cc0d wp-block-uagb-image--layout-default wp-block-uagb-image--effect-static wp-block-uagb-image--align-none"><figure class="wp-block-uagb-image__figure"><img decoding="async" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/chracter.png ,/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/chracter.png 780w, /wp-content/uploads/2026/03/chracter.png 360w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 480px) 150px" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/chracter.png" alt="Character Swap is CineV signature feature. It lets you replace characters your own in scenes to ensure professional quality and visual consistency across all clips. Swap characters while keeping your video's style and background perfectly consistent." class="uag-image-432" width="600" height="381" title="character-swap" loading="lazy" role="img"/></figure></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once the upload is complete, open a new project and assign your character to the Character slot. You can see this <strong>Character Swap</strong> in your each project.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">From here, you can choose camera angles, set a background, and pick a motion.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Making Your First Scene</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You do not need to know animation to get a result here.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In CineV, you start by entering a simple story or scene description. You can choose a location yourself, or let the AI decide. The <strong>CineV Storyboard Builder</strong> reads your story or prompt and selects appropriate locations and motions to build the first scene. If you want to adjust, open <strong>Edit in 3D</strong> to change motions directly, or click on a location and object to add local motion.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your first clip will use a small number of credits from your 300 starting balance. A short scene (a few seconds) costs a fraction of that, so you have room to experiment before spending anything.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Start with 300 free credits. No credit card needed. → Create your free account</p>
